## Regulatory and Legal Basis

### CMS Medicare Benefit Policy Manual Citations

1. **Chapter 15, §220.2 — Reasonable and Necessary Skilled Services:** Therapy services are covered when they require the skills of a qualified therapist and are reasonable and necessary for the treatment of the patient's condition.

2. **Chapter 15, §220.2(B) — Expectation of Improvement Not Required:** Coverage does not turn on the presence or absence of a patient's potential for improvement. Maintenance therapy qualifies for coverage if the specialized skills of a therapist are needed.

3. **Chapter 15, §220.3 — Coverage Not Dependent on Improvement Potential:** Per the *Jimmo v. Sebelius* settlement agreement (2013), the "Improvement Standard" is not a valid basis for denial. Even if a patient is maintaining function, skilled care is covered if a therapist's expertise is required.

### Applicable to This Denial

The denial asserts maximum benefit has been reached, yet:
- Standardized outcome measures show continued improvement
- Functional goals have not been fully achieved
- Treatment complexity requires ongoing clinical judgment
- Even if improvement plateaus, maintenance therapy requiring skilled care remains covered under *Jimmo v. Sebelius*

## Regulatory and Legal Basis

### CMS Citations

1. **Chapter 15, §220.2(B) — Skilled Services Defined:** Services are "skilled" when they require the expertise, knowledge, clinical judgment, and abilities of a qualified therapist. The determination is based on the complexity of the service and the condition of the patient.

2. **Chapter 15, §220.2(D) — Ongoing Assessment and Modification:** Skilled therapy includes ongoing assessment of the patient's response to treatment and modification of the treatment plan — activities that inherently require a licensed professional.

3. **Jimmo v. Sebelius (2013):** Maintenance therapy requiring the skills of a therapist is a covered service. "Non-skilled" cannot be applied simply because the exercises appear routine — the clinical judgment underlying their application is the skilled component.

### Why This Denial Is Incorrect

- The therapist made **real-time clinical decisions** during each session

- The **complexity of the patient's condition** required ongoing professional assessment
